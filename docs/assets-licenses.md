# Asset licenses

Record every font, icon set, image, illustration, animation, sound, 3D model and video used in the app, the store pages or the store listing, in the same PR that adds it. Only licenses that allow commercial use. Creative Commons "BY" licenses require visible attribution; "NC" (non-commercial) and "ND" (no derivatives) licenses are not allowed without approval.

Things that catch phone apps out:
- **Fonts**: a license for the web is not always a license for an app. Check that it allows embedding in an app you distribute (the SIL Open Font License does; many commercial licenses price it separately).
- **Icons**: Apple's SF Symbols may be used only in apps for Apple's platforms, so not in the Android app or on the site. Material Symbols (Apache-2.0) work everywhere.
- **The app icon and splash screen**: keep the source file and say who made it. An icon generated by AI still needs a record of the tool and its terms.
- **Store badges**: Apple's and Google's badges come with their own usage guidelines; use their artwork unmodified, linking to the real listing.
- **Store screenshots**: taken from our app with fake data. No photos of people without a model release, no other company's devices or logos beyond what the store allows.
- **Open-source code in the app**: its license notices ship inside the app, on an open-source licenses screen generated from the real dependencies (prompts/07-legal-review.md). Record here only assets; dependencies are listed by the license check in CI.
